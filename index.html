Re:closet -  How can we make sustainable living feel effortless?
It is a mobile application that reduces fast-fashion waste through clothing up-cycling and donation
PROBLEM & RESEARCH
Fast fashion and its textile waste are growing environmental challenges
The fashion industry generates 92 million tons of textile waste annually, while the average person in the U.S. discards 81.5 pounds of clothing each year.

To address this, we designed a prototype that encourages sustainable purchasing habits.
source: Earth.Org, “10 Concerning Fast Fahion Waste Statistics” (2023).
USER INSIGHTS
Women aged 20–30 with higher income are highly interested in sustainable fashion

Around 80%+ of respondents fit this profile

Around 98% expressed interest in eco-friendly actions
survey data collected via Google Forms
source: Earth.Org, “10 Concerning Fast Fahion Waste Statistics” (2023).

Campulse –  How can campus safety alerts became more actionable?
It is a mobile application that protects campus life through self-alert system
PROBLEM & RESEARCH
Import safety alerts get buried in crowded inboxes
UCSD delivers campus safety and event updates via official emails, but many students overlook them due to lengthy and the high volume of daily emails.
How effective do you think the current campus safety notificaiton system is?
Around 70% of respondents perceived the current campus safety alert system as ineffective.

The most common reasons were that safety alerts were buried among other emails (68.4%), users often overlooked the emails (47.4%), and it was difficult to identify the urgency or importance of the alerts (47.4%).
survey data collected via Google Forms
DESIGN GOALS

campus incident updates easier and faster
Create  an environment where campus communities actively share safety information

Spill the tea -  Why Gen Z trusts people over research?
It is a social discovery platform designed to discover authentic coffee spots through recommendations
PROBLEM & OPPORTUNITY
Sharing favorite cafes has become a growing social trend among Gen Z, with many discovering new places through friends and social media rather than traditional search platforms
However, existing platforms focus on ratings and text-based reviews, making it difficult to capture the atmosphere and overall experience that make each cafe or drink memorable.
This shift presents an opportunity to create a more engaging way to discover and recommend coffee and matcha spots.
DESIGN GOALS
Make
cafe recommendations more visual and trustworthy
Encourage
users to share their favorite coffee and tea spots
Help
users discover aesthetic places through recommentations
 USER INTERFACE
1
Select preferences
Users can select their favorite drink categories, such as matcha, coffee, and tea.
2
Log your drinks
Users can capture and log drinks in the in-app calendar, where each entry appears as a star-shaped marker.
3
Rank your favorites
Users can rank their top three drinks and save ratings, locations, and personal notes.
Main Navigation

My list

2

3
Community
Explore nearby cafes and drink recommendations shared by the community.
